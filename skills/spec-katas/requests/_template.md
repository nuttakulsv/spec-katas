# Request NN: Title

**Domain:** · **Difficulty:** Easy / Medium / Hard · **Practises:** two or three skills

## The request

The message as the stakeholder sends it. One to four sentences, vague in the realistic way.

## What you know

What the engineer knows going in: the product, the team, rough numbers. Under 120 words.

## Stakeholder

Who they are, what they believe the solution is, how they talk, what makes them change their mind.

## Hidden facts

Each fact, and the kind of question that unlocks it.

1. **Fact.** Unlocked by: ...

## Traps

What happens if the engineer builds the literal request.

## A good spec

A short model spec that uses the hidden facts.

## Rubric

Six to eight items: facts found, assumptions checked, spec quality.
